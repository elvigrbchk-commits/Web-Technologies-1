Jane Austen

1775-1817

About Jane Austen

Jane Austen (16 December 1775 – 18 July 1817) was an English writer known primarily for her six novels, which implicitly interpret, critique, and comment on the English landed gentry at the end of the 18th century.

Important events

  1. 1775 – Jane Austen was born in Steventon, England.
  2. 1811 – Sense and Sensibility was published.
  3. 1813 – Pride and Prejudice was published.
  4. 1815 – Emma was published.
  5. 1817 – Jane Austen died at the age of 41.

Key contributions

  • Development of the English novel.
  • Realistic portrayal of everyday life and relationships.
  • Use of irony and social commentary.
  • Influence on later English literature.
